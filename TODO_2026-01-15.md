### 7. LLM Integration (If Time Permits)

**Simple Implementation** (10 lines of code):
```python
# Add to sme_credit_explainability.py
import openai

def explain_credit_decision_with_llm(score, pd, features):
    """Generate AI-powered explanation of credit decision."""
    prompt = f"""
    Explain why this company has a credit score of {score} (PD: {pd:.2%}).
    Key features:
    - Revenue Growth: {features['revenue_growth']:.1%}
    - Debt-to-Asset Ratio: {features['debt_to_asset_ratio']:.1%}
    - Cash Flow Volatility: {features['cash_flow_volatility']:.2f}
    
    Provide a 2-sentence explanation in simple business language.
    """
    
    response = openai.ChatCompletion.create(
        model="gpt-4",
        messages=[{"role": "user", "content": prompt}]
    )
    return response.choices[0].message.content
```

**Add to app.py**:
```python
# In the Credit Memo section
if st.button("🤖 Ask AI to Explain"):
    with st.spinner("Generating AI explanation..."):
        explanation = explain_credit_decision_with_llm(new_score, new_pd, row)
        st.info(f"**AI Explanation**: {explanation}")
```
